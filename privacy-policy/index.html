Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy at probaseballkits.com

At ProBaseballKits.com, we respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website and make purchases.

Information We Collect
We collect personal information such as your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details when you place an order.
We also collect non-personal information like IP addresses, browser types, and site activity data to improve your experience.

How We Use Your Information
Your personal information is used to process orders, deliver products, and communicate with you regarding your purchases.
We may also send promotional offers if you’ve opted in.
We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except as necessary to fulfill your order or comply with legal requirements.

Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your data using secure methods. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee full protection against data breaches.

Cookies
We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website, such as remembering your preferences. You can manage your cookie settings through your browser.

Your Rights
You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data. If you wish to unsubscribe from our marketing emails, you can do so at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in the emails.

If you have any questions about our privacy practices, please contact us at contact@ProBaseballKits.com.
