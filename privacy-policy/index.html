Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy at mountainclimbpro.com

At MountainClimbPro.com, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect
We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, billing information, and shipping address when you place an order or subscribe to our newsletter. Non-personal data, such as browsing behavior, may also be collected through cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use your data to process orders, provide customer support, and send updates about promotions or new products. Non-personal data helps us improve our website and enhance your shopping experience.

3. Data Security
We implement robust security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted partners to facilitate payment processing and shipping.

5. Your Rights
You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@mountainclimbpro.com for assistance.
